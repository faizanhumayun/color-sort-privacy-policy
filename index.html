Privacy Policy for Color Sort

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Color Sort is a puzzle game published by Faizan Humayun. This policy explains how the app handles information. In short: the app does not collect any information about you.

Information we collect

None. Color Sort does not collect, store or share any personal information. We do not ask for a name, an email address, a phone number, a photo, a location or an account. There is no sign-in and no way to create a profile.

Internet access

The app does not use the internet. It does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send anything from your device to us or to anyone else. The game works completely offline.

Advertising and analytics

The app shows no advertisements. It contains no advertising networks, no analytics tools, and no third-party tracking software of any kind.

What is saved on the device

The app saves a few small settings on the phone or tablet itself, so the game can continue where you left off:

  • the highest level reached, and the stars earned on each level
  • whether sound effects are on or off
  • whether vibration is on or off
  • whether color-blind shapes are on or off
  • whether the note about limited undos and hints has been shown

This information stays on the device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Android backup

Android may copy those same settings into the device owner's own Google account as part of the phone's standard backup feature, so that progress returns if the phone is replaced. This is handled entirely by Android and Google, not by us, and we have no access to it. The phone's owner can turn backup off in the Android settings.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There is nothing for a parent to review, change or delete on our side.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases and no payments of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
